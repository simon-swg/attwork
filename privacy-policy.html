2. What Personal Data We Collect

Depending on how you use Attwork (as a customer, a tradesperson, or a visitor), we may collect:

Contact & identity data

  • Full name, phone number, email address
  • National ID / passport number and copies, where required for tradesperson verification
  • Trade licenses, certifications, or business registration documents (for tradespeople)

Location data

  • Home or business address
  • General location details (to match jobs with nearby tradespeople)

Form submission data

  • Details you provide when submitting an enquiry, job request, or registration form on the Platform
  • Job requirements, preferences, and history of enquiries
  • Reviews, ratings, or feedback you provide

Technical data

  • IP address, device type, browser type, operating system
  • Cookies and similar tracking technologies (see Section 7)
  • Website usage data and log files

We collect this data directly from you (when you fill out a form on the Platform) and, occasionally, from third parties (e.g. identity or license verification checks). We do not currently operate user log-in accounts — data is collected through submission forms.

4. How We Use Your Data

We use the personal data described above to:

  • Process and respond to your form submissions and enquiries
  • Match customers with suitable tradespeople based on trade, location, and availability
  • Verify tradesperson credentials and reduce fraud
  • Respond to customer support requests
  • Send updates related to your enquiry, job request, or submission
  • Send marketing communications, only if you have opted in
  • Analyze and improve Platform performance and user experience
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations in Thailand

We do not sell your personal data to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

5. Who We Share Your Data With

We may share your personal data with:

  • Other users of the Platform — e.g. a tradesperson may see a customer's job details and contact information necessary to complete a job; a customer may see a tradesperson's details and verification status.
  • Identity verification providers — to verify tradesperson identity and credentials, where applicable.
  • Data storage and productivity tools — such as Google Sheets/Google Workspace, which we use to record and manage form submissions.
  • Website hosting and IT service providers — to host and secure the Platform.
  • Analytics and communication providers — should we introduce tools such as website analytics or email/SMS delivery services in the future.
  • Government or regulatory authorities — where required by Thai law, court order, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Attwork, our users, or the public.
  • Professional advisors — such as lawyers or auditors, where necessary.

All third parties who process data on our behalf are required to protect it in line with this Privacy Policy and applicable law.

Cross-Border Data Transfers

Some of the tools we use to manage submitted data — for example, Google Sheets/Google Workspace — may store or process data on servers located outside Thailand. Where this occurs, we ensure the receiving country provides an adequate level of data protection, or we put appropriate safeguards in place, as required under the PDPA.

6. How Long We Keep Your Data

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to:

  • Fulfil the purposes described in this policy,
  • Meet legal, accounting, or regulatory requirements, and
  • Resolve disputes and enforce our agreements.

Retention periods:

  • General submission/contact data: for as long as you are an active customer or contractor engaging with Attwork, plus 3 years after that relationship ends
  • Identity verification documents: for as long as the contractor or customer is actively working with us

After these periods, data is securely deleted or anonymized.

If we introduce such tools, you will be able to manage or disable non-essential cookies through your browser settings or a cookie consent banner on the site. Disabling some cookies may affect Platform functionality. This section will be updated accordingly if and when cookies are introduced.

8. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal data, including:

  • Restricting access to personal data to those who need it

  • Regular review of our data handling practices

However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your Rights Under the PDPA

As a data subject under Thai law, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Delete or anonymize your data, in certain circumstances
  • Withdraw consent at any time (where processing is based on consent)
  • Object to certain processing, including for direct marketing
  • Restrict processing in certain circumstances
  • Data portability — request your data in a structured, commonly used format
  • Lodge a complaint with the Office of the Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC) of Thailand if you believe your rights have been violated

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@attwork.com. We will respond within the timeframe required by the PDPA.

10. Children's Data

We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors without appropriate parental or guardian consent. If we become aware that we have collected data from a minor without proper consent, we will take steps to delete it.
